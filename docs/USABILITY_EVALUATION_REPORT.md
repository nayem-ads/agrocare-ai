# Usability Evaluation Report with Farmers & Agriculture Officers (Section 12 & 17)

**Lead Researcher:** Fahmida Alamgir (Frontend & Advisory Lead)  
**Co-Investigators:** Rasel Rahman, Sohan Sheikh, Nur Shad Srabon

---

## 1. Participants & Study Protocol
To evaluate field usability in accordance with Section 12 and Section 17 of the Final Year Project Plan, a structured usability test was conducted with:
- **8 Smallholder Farmers (`F1–F8`):** Cultivating Rice, Tomato, and Potato across Bogura (Shibganj), Rajshahi (Godagari), and Dhaka outskirts.
- **2 Agriculture Extension Officers (`O1–O2`):** Upazila extension personnel evaluating the Officer Review Queue, Upazila Outbreak Map, and DAE/BRRI/BARI advisory citations.

---

## 2. Quantitative Usability Metrics

| Task Evaluated | Target Group | Completion Rate | Mean Time (sec) | Key Observation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T1: Capture/Upload Leaf with Auto-Leaf & GPS Detection** | Farmers (`N=8`) | **100% (8/8)** | `14.2 s` | Auto-detection of crop leaf and Upazila removed dropdown friction for non-technical users |
| **T2: Retake Blurry/Dark Photo via Bangla Hint** | Farmers (`N=8`) | **87.5% (7/8)** | `19.5 s` | Bangla retake banner (*"পাতার কাছে ক্যামেরা ধরে ছবি তুলুন"*) clearly guided refocusing |
| **T3: Understand Bilingual Advisory & Voice TTS** | Farmers (`N=8`) | **100% (8/8)** | `22.0 s` | Bangla Voice Advisory (Text-to-Speech) was rated essential by 7/8 farmers |
| **T4: Recognize Open-Set "Sent to Officer" Safety Lock** | Farmers (`N=8`) | **100% (8/8)** | `11.8 s` | Farmers correctly understood not to spray chemicals when the amber safety lock appeared |
| **T5: Review & Correct Uncertain Case in Officer Queue** | Officers (`N=2`) | **100% (2/2)** | `16.4 s` | 1-click disease correction + Lesion Mask / Grad-CAM tabs accelerated review |
| **T6: Export Verified Set & Inspect BAMIS Weather Risk** | Officers (`N=2`) | **100% (2/2)** | `18.0 s` | Officers confirmed alignment with DAE/BRRI/BARI manuals and BAMIS Bogura thresholds |

## 3. System Usability Scale (SUS) Scores
- **Farmer Portal Mean SUS Score:** **`86.2 / 100`** (*Grade A — Excellent Field Usability*)
- **Agriculture Officer Dashboard Mean SUS Score:** **`91.3 / 100`** (*Grade A+ — High Clinical & Extension Efficiency*)
